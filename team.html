Meet the team

My Medical Daybook is made by Mark 1 Apps Ltd, a small company run by Mark and Shelley Brown.

Mark Brown

Founder and director

Mark served 24 years in the British Army with the Royal Electrical and Mechanical Engineers, from 1999 to 2023, leaving as a Warrant Officer Class 1. He spent his last twelve years designing, delivering and running training: lead training delivery instructor, senior training analyst, senior operations manager, and finally senior training manager. In that last role he was accountable for five learning programmes and led a team of training analysts and evaluation officers.

Since leaving the Army he has led learning and development in industry, and now heads national training for a UK company. He is a Chartered Manager and a Fellow of the Chartered Management Institute, and holds the CIPD Level 5 Diploma in Learning and Development and a degree in electronic systems engineering.

Daybook began as his own way of keeping track between appointments. Read the story

Shelley Brown, Assoc CIPD

Director

Shelley has spent twenty years in learning, talent and resourcing, much of it in global roles. At Hewlett Packard Enterprise she ran talent and learning programmes across Europe, the Americas and Asia Pacific, and led the roll-out across Europe, the Middle East and Africa of its award-winning Management Excellence Programme.

She went on to manage product development projects at BCS, The Chartered Institute for IT, then led apprenticeship, learning delivery and training teams in industry, including training across EMEA for a global software company. She is an Associate Member of the CIPD and an experienced project manager in heavily regulated industries.

At Daybook she is the sounding board and the dedicated grown-up: she tests every change and keeps the plans honest.
