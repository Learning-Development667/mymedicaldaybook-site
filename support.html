Support

Daybook is in Beta. Mark and Shelley read every message.

Get in touch

Email support@mymedicaldaybook.co.uk. Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead; a screenshot helps. Never put anyone's health details in an email unless you need to.

Put Daybook on your phone or computer

There are three ways in, all the same Daybook. The same buttons are on the home page under Get Daybook.

  • Android: open Daybook on Google Play, tap Join to join the open Beta, then Install.
  • iPhone or iPad: open Daybook on TestFlight. TestFlight is Apple's own app for trying apps before they reach the App Store; it installs first if you need it. Then tap Accept and Install.
  • Any other device, or if you prefer: open Daybook in your browser. On an iPhone or iPad in Safari, tap the Share button, then Add to Home Screen. On a Mac in Safari, choose File, Add to Dock. On Android in Chrome, tap the three dots, then Add to Home screen.

Questions people ask

Who can see what I record? Only the people in your circle, each with the access you gave them. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Can I use it for something other than cancer? Yes. Under Settings, tick what applies: cancer treatment, Crohn's and colitis, or Something else for chronic pain or any other long-term condition, and type what it is. The report works for any team you see between appointments.

Does it give medical advice? No. It keeps the record and does simple sums. Your care team makes the decisions. In an emergency call 999; for urgent advice call 111.

Medicine reminders are not arriving. Reminders are switched on under Settings in Daybook. In a browser on an iPhone, they only work once Daybook is on the Home Screen. Check the phone's own notification settings too.

Delete your My Medical Daybook account or your records

You can do all of this yourself in the Daybook app, with no need to ask anyone.

Delete one record. Open the entry, document or recording and tap Delete. It is removed straight away.

Delete your account and your whole care circle (the patient, or whoever set the circle up):

  1. Open Daybook and tap More.
  2. Tap Your records.
  3. If you want a copy first, tap Download all the records.
  4. Tap Delete this care circle, then Delete the circle for good.

This erases everything in the circle (every entry, medicine, document, recording, note and reminder) and your sign-in, all at once.

Leave a circle you were invited to. Under More, Your records, anyone else taps Leave and delete my sign-in. What they logged stays in the patient's records, because those are the patient's.

What is kept, and for how long. Nothing stays in Daybook once deleted. The database's daily backups are kept for 14 days and then removed, so a deleted circle is gone from them within 14 days. Google keeps standard server logs for a limited period. Daily usage counts hold no names or records.

If you cannot get into the app, email support@mymedicaldaybook.co.uk from the address you signed up with and it will be done within 30 days, usually much sooner.
