Before you start

You need a Beta code.

The Beta is by invitation. Whoever told you about Daybook, a nurse, a clinic, a friend or a message from Mark, gives you a short code. You type it once, when you create your account.

No code, or lost it? Email support@mymedicaldaybook.co.uk and say who told you about Daybook. Mark will send one.

Ten minutes, once

Three steps.

  1. 1

    Open Daybook and create your account

    Open Daybook in Safari on an iPhone or iPad, in Chrome on an Android phone, or in any browser on a Mac. Tap Create an account. It asks for your first name, whether you are the patient, a carer or family, an email address and a password, your Beta code, and who told you about Daybook.

    If someone in your circle has already set Daybook up, ask them for an invite link instead. It joins you to their circle, no code needed.

  2. 2

    Add it to your Home Screen

    This is the step people miss, and the one that makes Daybook open like an app and send medicine reminders.

    iPhone or iPad: in Safari, tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen, scroll the list and tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

    Android: in Chrome, tap the three dots at the top right, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

    Mac: in Safari, File, then Add to Dock.

  3. 3

    Log your first day

    Open Daybook from your Home Screen. Add your medicines under Meds, do the morning or evening check-in on Today, and log a reading or two. Under More, then Settings, turn on reminders and invite the people who help.

    On the first day, Daybook asks eight quick questions about how setting up went. Two minutes, and it tells Mark more than any amount of guessing.

What the Beta is for

Tell Mark what you think.

A button at the top of Today says exactly that. Tap it whenever something is confusing, wrong, or simply not what you expected, and say it in words or by voice; add a screenshot if it helps. It goes straight to Mark, with which screen you were on.

Prefer email? support@mymedicaldaybook.co.uk. Mark's phone number is inside the app, for anyone who would rather talk.

Never put anyone's health details in an email or a message unless you need to. Everything you log in Daybook stays in your circle; see the privacy policy.
