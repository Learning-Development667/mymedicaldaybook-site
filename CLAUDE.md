# The Daybook website

The public site for My Medical Daybook at **https://mymedicaldaybook.co.uk** (GitHub Pages from `main`, root; the `CNAME` file names the domain, since 18 October 2026; `www.` and the old github.io address redirect to it). DNS is in Mark's Cloudflare account (markbrown667@gmail.com): four A records for the apex to GitHub's 185.199.108.153 to .111.153 and CNAMEs `www` and `app` to learning-development667.github.io, all DNS only (grey cloud: a proxied record stops GitHub issuing the certificate), beside the email records (MX, the DKIM, DMARC and SPF TXT rows, and Resend's send and rsend CNAMEs), which must be left alone. The app is a separate repository, `Learning-Development667/Mark_Medical`; this site only links to it. Built 11 October 2026 from the website brief (Claude Docs, "Daybook website brief") and the home page words ("Daybook home page words") in the brand guide's plum and apricot; **rebuilt 18 October 2026 (v3) in the app's own look**, because the first thing people see should look like the thing they are about to install (Mark's brief that day: the demos, the Beta behind the code, guides showing the logging and the reports, everything focused on the time between appointments, "very premium"). The brand guide 1.2 (Mark's Branding folder) still rules the words and the mark; its plum palette is retired on this site in favour of the app's.

## Rules
- Plain HTML, CSS and JS. No frameworks, no build step, no cookies, no analytics scripts, no forms that post anywhere. GitHub Pages serves pages only; the one call the site makes is the Beta code box asking the app's bridge (`GET /beta-info?code=`), which answers whether a code is good and whose it is, nothing more.
- All work on `main`. Bump `?v=` on `css/site.css` and `js/site.js` in every page when either changes (and the `guide.json?v=` in `js/site.js` when the guide is recopied). Run `node --check js/site.js` before committing.
- UK English. No em dashes. Body text 18px, 4.5:1 contrast, 44px tap targets at least, WCAG 2.2 AA with zero axe violations, nothing overflows at 320px. Every picture has alt text.
- Look (v3): the app's tokens in `css/site.css`: page #F6F4EF, ink #17263A, slate #4B5B6B, muted #56667A, teal #18727A for text and #1E7F86 for fills, terracotta #C2703D for the one warm accent, green #2D7354; dark page #0F1B24, surface #172633, teal #5CC3C9. The watercolour wash behind the whole page (`body::before`, `media/wash-light.jpg` and `wash-dark.jpg`, the app's own baked pictures, so every text token keeps 4.5:1 on them; see the app's CLAUDE.md, v229). Fraunces 600 for headings and the brand, Inter for everything else. Headings in ink; teal for kickers, links and buttons. Every section is either on the wash or `.solid` (the surface at 72%).
- Mark 1 Apps appears in the footer only: the line-art mark on a paper chip, the copyright line, company number and registered office. No page, section or headline about the company.
- No health data, ever. The report pictures and the guide frames come from the app's in-memory previews (made-up people: Sam and Jo).
- Quotes go live only in the person's own approved words. Nothing made up.

## Files
- `index.html`: the home page, one scroll: the hero (the app's opener clip, `media/opener.mp4` with its first frame as the poster, "My Medical" over "Daybook", the strapline, Free in Beta, Try the demo and Join the Beta; along its foot a full-width frosted-glass band, "Scroll down to see everything Daybook does" ("Scroll down for more" on a phone), with the arrow centred under the words, bobbing and fading slowly, and a soft sweep of light across the glass every few seconds, all off under reduced motion; tapping it goes to `#why`; the picture stops short of the screen's foot so the next section peeks in. Mark chose this on 18 October 2026 over a row of four teaser cards, which looked jarring, and over a paper-coloured strip), why Daybook exists (the walk film), how it started, **What it does between appointments** (`#does`: six cards with real screens from `guide/`, then six more things in a list), **See it work** (`#guide`: the app's own Guide played in a phone frame, twelve topics, the story first), **The reports** (`#reports`: five reports as real pages, one at a time), the circle (Hayley's and Keith's words), **Try it** (`#try`: Cancer treatment and Crohn's or colitis open the app's previews, `?preview=cancer` and `?preview=ibd`, two cards side by side; the site says nothing about menopause since 20 October 2026, Mark: not ready, focus on cancer and Crohn's), **Join the Beta** (`#beta`: the code box), private by design, the close, the footer.
- `beta.html`: Join the Beta, where the footer's link and old links lead: the code box first, the promise (free throughout the Beta; Beta circles keep the full app free for a year after the App Store, with everything logged; Mark's decision of 11 October 2026), the three steps, how to give feedback, the two demos.
- `privacy.html`, `terms.html`: the app's own privacy and terms text, copied from `Mark_Medical/index.html` templates (`tpl-privacy`, `tpl-terms`) with `h3` made `h2` and the contact span filled. When the app's text changes, copy it again.
- `support.html`: how to get help, install steps, common questions, how to delete records.
- `css/site.css`, `js/site.js`: the hero clip plays once and rests on its last frame; the walk film loops and pauses out of view; `.reveal` blocks rise in as they arrive (`html.js` is set by an inline script, so without JavaScript nothing is hidden); the stills drift slower than the page (`.pic.parallax`); the report pages fan in; the guide player (`#g-phone`, `#g-chips`, the ring from each step's recorded tap, Previous, Play or Pause, Next, the steps in words; plays only while on screen); the report chooser (`#r-chips`); the Beta code box (`#beta-code`: tidied to letters and digits, checked with the bridge, a good code shows "Your code from {label} is good.", then **What will you use Daybook for?** with Cancer treatment and Crohn's or colitis (since 20 October 2026, the app's v245), each linking to `https://app.mymedicaldaybook.co.uk/?beta=CODE&for=cancer|ibd` so the app saves the choice and its set-up does not ask again, and the three steps; a `?beta=CODE` on the site's own address fills and checks it). - `media/`: the opener clip and poster, the two wash pictures, the walk film (1280 mp4 and webm, 1920 mp4, posters), the five stills at 800 and 1600 (jpg and webp), and the five reports' pages (`report-{notes,ibd,pain,food,mood}-{n}.webp` and `.jpg`, 1000px wide, from the previews; the old `report-1`/`-2` are kept for nothing and can go). `icons/`: the app's own icon (192, 512, the 180 touch icon, 32 and 64 favicons) and `mark1apps-line.png` for the footer chip.

- **Search** (19 October 2026, Mark: typing the address into Google's AI box gave an overview instead of the site): every page has a canonical link, `og:url`, `og:site_name` and an absolute `og:image` (a relative one is ignored by link previews), the home page carries WebSite and Organization JSON-LD so Google shows "My Medical Daybook" as the site name, and the root has `robots.txt` (everything allowed, points at the sitemap) and `sitemap.xml` (the five pages; add a page here when one is added).
