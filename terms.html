1. What Daybook is

My Medical Daybook (Daybook for short) is a personal record-keeping tool for a patient and the people looking after them, made and run by Mark 1 Apps Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (company number 17510025). Contact: support@mymedicaldaybook.co.uk.

It is not a medical device, it gives no medical advice, and it does not replace your doctors, nurses or pharmacist. Everything it shows, including the summary lines, the flags, the food estimates and the AI explanations, is there to help you talk to your care team, not to decide anything for you. If you are worried about a symptom, contact your care team or NHS 111. In an emergency call 999.

One sign-in per person. Keep your password private; you are responsible for what is done with your sign-in. The household owner decides who is invited and with what access, and can remove them. By inviting someone you confirm that the patient agrees to them seeing the records.

They remain yours. You give Mark 1 Apps Ltd permission only to store them and show them to your household, as the privacy policy describes. You are responsible for what you enter and for checking it is right, especially medicine doses and times.

4. Using it fairly

Daybook is for personal, non-commercial use. Do not try to get into another household's data, probe or overload the service, or use it for anything unlawful. Do not enter anyone's information without their agreement.

5. Recordings

"Record the answer" and "Record the appointment" record people speaking. Ask them first, every time; the app asks you to confirm that you have. You are responsible for any recording you make and for what you do with it.

6. Daybook Assistant (the AI features)
